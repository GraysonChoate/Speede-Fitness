Isokinetic Training: Building Strength
Builds strength in less time
Isokinetic machines that can customize resistance to the user are proven to offer faster increases in strength and muscle mass.
Increases bone density
Compared to traditional weight lifting, isokinetic training offers a safer way to lift heavy loads and stimulates the production of healthy bones.
Helps prevent future injuries
Isokinetic training fixes muscle imbalances that remain after injuries, strengthening affected areas to help prevent re-injury.
