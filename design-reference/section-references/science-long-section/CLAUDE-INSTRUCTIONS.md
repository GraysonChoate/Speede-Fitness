# Science Section: Continuous Signal Journey

Treat this entire long section as one continuous visual system with three scroll
beats. Preserve all existing copy, cards, graphs, typography, spacing, and layout.
Do not add visible words, numerals, labels, legends, or interface elements.

## Shared Rules

- All atmosphere stays beneath content with `pointer-events: none`.
- Maintain at least 40px of clear space around copy, cards, graphs, and controls.
- Use the existing center and outer gutters as the paths for the signal.
- Keep the treatment unmistakably visible at normal screenshot size.
- Primary traces: 34-44% opacity.
- Secondary green-gray traces: 24-34% opacity.
- Dotted traces and dot fields: 18-28% opacity.
- Bright lime is reserved for a few small signal nodes.

## Opening Beat: Converge

- Route three traces from the empty upper-left band and three from the
  upper-right band.
- Curve them into the narrow gutter between the copy and live graph.
- Combine solid, dotted, graphite, and desaturated-green paths.
- Let the six traces converge into one bright node near the lower half of the
  opening viewport.
- Split the traces again before the two lower cards begin.

## Middle Beat: Split

- Continue one narrow signal spine through the gap between the two cards.
- Below the cards, split it left and right along the empty horizontal gutter.
- Bend each branch downward outside the large graph card.
- Add incomplete corner brackets around the two-card cluster.
- Use fading dot fields in the far-left and far-right margins.

## Curve Beat: Frame

- Turn the split branches into three perimeter rails on each side of the large
  strength-curve card.
- Route the rails around the card's outside edge without entering it.
- Mix two solid rails with one dotted rail.
- Add diagonal hatching in the two lower exterior corners.
- Use one restrained node at each lower corner to show the signal reconnecting.

## Motion

- Opening: send one glint from each upper side into the central convergence node.
- Middle: after convergence, send the glint left and right beneath the card pair.
- Curve: let the two glints travel down the outer rails and meet at the lower edge.
- Complete the full journey over 12-16 seconds and repeat only after a pause.
- Keep the rails stationary; animate glints, a 4-8px dot-field drift, and small
  node brightness changes.
- Pause each beat while its viewport is offscreen.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, display the complete static system.

## Hard Limits

- No decoration may cross text, graphs, card interiors, or controls.
- No added words, numerals, scales, specifications, prices, or mode names.
- No neon circuitry, fluid waves, topographic fields, bokeh, decorative orbs,
  or unrelated visual motifs.
