# The Number: Compression Frame

Use the supplied visual reference as the exact art direction. Preserve the
existing section's copy, numeral, typography, spacing, comparison rows, colors,
and layout.

Do not add visible words, labels, numerals, legends, specifications, or UI.
Everything added to the section must be abstract.

## Protected Zones

- Nothing may cross or sit over the eyebrow, large numeral, unit line, headline,
  paragraph, or comparison rows.
- Keep at least 40px of clear space around all existing content.
- Place every atmospheric layer beneath the content with `pointer-events: none`.

## Visual Treatment

- Build an open rectangular compression frame around the numeral block using
  four strong corner brackets. Keep the middle of every side open.
- Add a second, fainter inset bracket at each corner to create dimensional depth.
- Introduce six horizontal pressure bands from the far-left gutter. The bands
  should shorten as they approach the center and fade before reaching the numeral.
- Add matching shorter pressure bands inside the central gutter between the
  numeral and the right-hand copy.
- Add one broken vertical calibration rail at the far-left edge with clearly
  visible varied tick lengths.
- Add a fading dot field behind the left gutter and sparse vertical load bars in
  the central gutter.
- Place a few dim particles around the frame and one small lime glint at the
  lower-right corner bracket.

## Visibility

- Keep the treatment unmistakably visible at normal screenshot size without zooming.
- Use primary bracket opacity around 45-55%.
- Use secondary green-gray brackets around 22-30%.
- Keep pressure bands between 26-42% opacity.
- Keep dust and particles between 12-22%.
- Bright lime is reserved for the single small glint.
- Fade every atmospheric layer before it reaches the right-hand copy and rows.
- Do not approve the treatment if the arcs disappear when the full section is
  viewed at ordinary laptop brightness.

## Motion

- Let the pressure bands contract inward by 8-12px over 3-4 seconds, then return.
- Send one tiny glint down the left calibration rail every 9-12 seconds.
- Let the lower-right corner node brighten briefly when the pressure bands reach
  their inward position.
- Keep the brackets stationary.
- Stop motion while the section is offscreen.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, show the static treatment only.

## Hard Limits

- No added words, numerals, scales, measurements, specifications, or prices.
- No circles, rings, radar interfaces, bokeh, fluid waves,
  circuitry, topographic patterns, or decoration over content.
- Do not modify existing page content or introduce new photography.
