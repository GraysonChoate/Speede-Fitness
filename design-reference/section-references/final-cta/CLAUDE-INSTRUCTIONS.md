# Final CTA: Force-Event Treatment

Use the supplied reference as the visual target. Preserve the existing headline, form, reassurance copy, footer, navigation, spacing, and responsive structure.

## Core idea

The final CTA is the page's release of stored force. Earlier sections feel measured and analytical; this section should feel charged. Exterior telemetry compresses from both sides, resolves around the CTA, and releases through a lower activation band.

## Hard rules

- Do not add any words, numbers, labels, legends, icons, or callout markers.
- Do not place traces, dots, glow, or grain over the headline, form fields, button, reassurance text, navigation, or footer.
- Do not change the CTA copy or form.
- Use only `#080A09`, `#0E120F`, `#B2FF59`, `#8FD63F`, `#F4F7F0`, and `#9AA39A`.
- The treatment must be clearly visible at 100% scale on an ordinary laptop display.

## Visual layers

1. **Upper telemetry canopy**
   - Place a dense but shallow dot field and four broken horizontal traces above the headline.
   - The traces shorten toward the center and terminate around one small illuminated node.
   - End the entire canopy at least 56px above the headline.

2. **Side pressure banks**
   - Fill the far-left and far-right negative space with visible dot matrices.
   - Route seven hard-edged traces from each side toward the CTA.
   - The traces narrow and converge, but stop outside the central protected zone.
   - Use straight segments and shallow angles. No fluid curves.

3. **Broken CTA brackets**
   - Add four strong corner brackets around the combined headline-and-form area.
   - Add one fainter dashed echo outside them.
   - Never draw a continuous box.

4. **Activation field**
   - Build a broad radial graphite-to-lime exposure underneath the form.
   - Add a horizontal scan band, fine grain, and broken traces below the reassurance copy.
   - The field should read as emitted energy, not a decorative gradient blob.

5. **Convergence release**
   - Below the form, two traces step inward, flatten briefly, then exit toward the section boundary.
   - Use only six to eight illuminated nodes across the complete section.

## Visibility

- Primary pressure rails and CTA brackets: 68–82% effective opacity.
- Side dot fields: 44–60%.
- Secondary traces and bracket echoes: 34–48%.
- Activation field and grain: 24–40%.
- Keep lime glow tight; no large neon haze.
- If the side pressure banks and four main brackets are not immediately visible in a full-page screenshot, increase contrast.

## Motion

- On section entry, reveal the side dot fields over 500ms.
- Draw the outer pressure rails inward over 900–1200ms with a slight 60ms stagger.
- Allow one pulse to travel from each edge to the nearest bracket corner.
- Bring the upper traces toward the center node once, then hold.
- Let the activation field brighten by 8–12% as the CTA enters the center of the viewport.
- On button hover or input focus, send one fast pulse through the lower convergence traces and increase the nearest node glow for 280ms.
- Do not loop every element. After the entry sequence, only one faint side pulse should repeat every 8–12 seconds.
- Use linear or restrained ease-out timing. No waves, liquid morphing, elastic movement, particle explosions, or constant flicker.

## Reduced motion and mobile

- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, show the composed static state with no traveling pulses.
- On mobile, retain the upper canopy, four main brackets, lower activation field, and three pressure rails per side.
- Remove secondary bracket echoes and reduce dot density by roughly 35%.
- Maintain at least 28px of clear space around all CTA content.
